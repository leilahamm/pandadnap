The dataset

Every week, readers submit captions for a New Yorker cartoon, and other readers rate them as not funny, somewhat funny or funny. We use the public archive of this contest.

What is in a row

The caption text, how many voters rated it not funny, somewhat funny or funny, its average rating from 1 to 3, its number of votes and its rank in the contest. For 240 of the 384 contests, hand-written descriptions of the cartoon are also available (Hessel et al.).

What to keep in mind

Votes are not spread evenly: the contest shows better-rated captions more often, so a caption with few votes has an unreliable score. The median caption has 84 votes, and we keep captions with at least 20 votes. Captions are short, about 10 words on average.
